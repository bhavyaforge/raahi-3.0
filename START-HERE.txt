RAAHI 3.0 — how to run it
=========================

There are two copies of the same app in this folder. Use whichever you like.


THE EASY ONE — a single file
----------------------------

    python3 raahi.py

That is the whole thing. Everything is inside that one file: the server,
the web pages, the crack detection, the lot. It does not matter which
folder your Terminal is in — you can even type "python3 " and then drag
the file into the Terminal window instead of typing the path.

Your browser opens at http://localhost:8000
Stop it with Control + C.

Readings are saved in a folder called "data", created next to raahi.py.
Delete that folder to start clean.


THE NORMAL ONE — the source folder
----------------------------------

    cd ~/Desktop/raahi3.0
    python3 server.py

Same app, but split into files your team can read and edit:

    web/index.html, web/styles.css, web/app.js, web/hero.js
    raahi_backend/*.py
    tools/*.py

web/hero.js is the road on the front page — sky, sun, asphalt, the
reflection down the tarmac and the crack, all drawn in code. There is no
image file to swap; change the numbers at the top of that file.


TRY IT WITHOUT GOING OUTSIDE
----------------------------

    python3 raahi.py demo

Writes eighteen photographs of a crack that grows a little each morning,
day 1 to day 18, each one carrying real GPS in its metadata. On the
CAPTURE tab press "CHOOSE PHOTOS" and select the whole folder at once —
they go up in order, with a line each saying what happened.

Photo 1 says "New spot recorded". Photo 2 onward says "Same spot as
before" and explains why — the metres from the recorded position, the
camera heading, the scene match.

Then look at GROWTH, SEAL LIST and REPORT. The crack grows about 1.4 mm a
day, the lead time comes out around 28 days, and the seal list ranks it by

    growth rate  x  rainfall forecast  x  traffic

with every term and where it came from printed beside the answer.


THE SHUTTER PRESSES ITSELF
--------------------------

Press "Capture photo" and the camera watches its own preview. When
something crack-shaped holds still in front of it for three frames, it
takes the photograph by itself — nobody has to press anything, which is
the point when the camera is bolted inside a truck.

A webcam frame has no GPS of its own, so the app writes one in: the
position, the heading, the accuracy and the time go into the file's own
hidden EXIF at the moment of capture, exactly the way a phone does it.
Open one of the files in data/photos afterwards in any EXIF viewer and
the coordinates are there.


IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
------------------------

Front page showing big percentages, or a headline across the road image?
You are looking at an older copy. The front page should be a wordless
sunrise over a road, with the writing below it.

Camera button does nothing? The camera needs the server — it will not
work if you double-click index.html. Uploading a photo always works, and
an uploaded phone photo carries GPS, which a webcam frame does not.

Nothing to install. Python 3 only. Nothing leaves your machine.
