What this prototype does

Photograph a crack. The app measures it, records where the photo was taken, and recognises that spot again the next time somebody stands there. Once the same crack has been photographed twice, it has a growth rate — and a date by which it needs sealing.

WORKING IN THIS BUILD

  • Finds the crack in a photograph without being told where it is
  • Follows every bend and reports length in millimetres
  • Reads GPS out of the photo's own EXIF, or off the device when capturing live
  • Decides whether today's photo is yesterday's crack — position, camera heading and a fingerprint of the scene itself
  • Fits a growth rate across passes and dates the threshold crossing
  • Reports lead time: days between first sighting and predicted failure
  • Scores a damage detector per class, on a named split, sample size stated

NOT IN THIS BUILD, AND WE SAY SO

  • No trained damage classifier. Detection is classical image processing, not a model — the class labels on the seal list come from whoever typed them
  • No detection score to quote yet. The DETECTION QUALITY tab stays empty until a real model is evaluated against real labels
  • Runs on a laptop or a phone browser, not on a truck
  • Daylight, close range, camera roughly overhead. Not heavy rain
  • Absolute length carries a small constant bias. Growth is a subtraction, so the bias cancels — that is why growth is the number we report

Everything is stored in data/ — one SQLite file and the photos, byte for byte as the camera wrote them. Delete the folder to start clean. Nothing leaves the machine.

The seal list

Built only from readings actually taken on this machine. A spot with one photograph has no rate, so it gets no date — it sits at the bottom until it is photographed again.

LEAD TIME — THE FIGURE NOBODY PUBLISHES

Lead time is the gap between the day a crack was first seen and the day it is predicted to cross the sealing threshold. It only exists if somebody photographs the same square metre of road more than once, which is why no Indian road authority reports it today.
